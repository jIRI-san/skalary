# Intent

> Preliminary context captured from the `bcece1` epic discussion on 2026-08-14. `/cip` must confirm and refine it.

## Goal

Make `/cip` capture the operator's real desired outcome and end-user experience, then agree the high-level program shape before implementation work begins.

## Desired outcome

The planning interview actively rephrases and confirms operator intent, identifies the complete implementation and its MVP, preserves important original discussion, and produces an approved concise Mermaid-backed design with optional call stacks where they clarify behavior. The resulting plan is complete but organized into MVP-first vertical phases.

## Success signals

- The operator recognizes their own goal, user experience, success signals, non-goals, and done bar in the plan assets.
- Important discussion points, decisions, uncertainty, and rejected alternatives survive beyond chat scrollback.
- Phase 1 delivers an end-to-end MVP; later phases add usable increments until the complete outcome is delivered.
- Program structure and important call flows are reviewed before costly implementation.

## Non-goals

- Reducing `/cip` to an MVP-only or partial plan.
- Writing detailed implementation requirements during `/cep` decomposition.
- Treating a Mermaid diagram or call stack as a substitute for typed acceptance evidence.

## Definition of done

- A newly drafted plan carries confirmed intent provenance, an approved high-level design, explicit MVP boundaries, and a full vertical route from first usable slice to complete delivery.

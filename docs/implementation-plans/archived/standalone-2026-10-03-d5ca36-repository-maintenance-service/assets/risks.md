# Risks

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Steps |
|----|------|------------|--------|------------|-------|
| RISK-1 | Enhanced intent discovery exists on another unintegrated branch, so a summary is mistaken for an available API or missing snippets hide relevant context. | High | Medium | Inspect current integrated source; reuse current inventory and bounded intent intake. Disclose search/epic-context limitations when snippets are unavailable; stop only for incompatible current helpers or unresolved needed context. No automatic merge or duplicate index. | 1.1 |
| RISK-2 | Broad human-source discovery overwhelms context or missing history is reported as absence. | High | Medium | Inventory first, relevant index snippets, max-three historical artifacts per request, explicit inaccessible/unread/refused sources and operator-selected further scope; no automated batching around the cap. | 1.1, 1.2 |
| RISK-3 | Generic preferences, unfinished plans or superseded historical intent are mislabeled defects/drift. | Medium | High | Require current location/trigger/impact/evidence, exceptions/counter-evidence and scope overlap; distinguish alignment questions, unfinished work and optional improvements. Stop for unresolved expectation conflicts. | 1.2, 2.1 |
| RISK-4 | Dead-code advice misses indirect/manual/exported/external use and causes a destructive corrective plan. | High | High | Enumerate supported entry points and dynamic mechanisms; present evidence limits, retain manual tools, and never delete during `/rcs`. Escalate unknown supported-call boundaries instead of asserting deadness. | 2.1, 2.2 |
| RISK-5 | Acceptance/won't-fix becomes an unscoped exemption or silently amends protected criteria/contracts. | Medium | High | Explicit scope/rationale/revisit condition, preserve original intent, recheck changed basis, and route criteria/contract changes to owning approval flows. A cleanup entry is advisory. | 2.2 |
| RISK-6 | Record writes leak secrets, follow links, erase human decisions, grow beyond bounded intake, or imply full coverage. | Medium | High | Fixed physical path, shared guards, input/output 128-KiB cap, preserved prose, no unseen-entry closure, disposable refusal tests. Stop visibly without truncation/rollover or modifying original on failed publication. | 1.2, 3.1 |
| RISK-7 | Archive or planning handoff claims success despite missing completion evidence, absent tools or interrupted action. | Medium | High | Recheck current gates, explicit per-action approval, inspect actual installed archive route, and link/record actual successful results only. Pending/blocked remains visible; no autonomous implementation launch for archive. | 2.2 |
| RISK-8 | Packaging/tests validate dogfood but fail foreign consumers or overstate semantic audit quality. | Medium | High | Manifest-declared reusable closure plus foreign-consumer fixtures, focused deterministic helper/structural checks and disposable cited scenario traces. No measured-gain claim or routine paid/full suite. | 3.1, 3.2 |
